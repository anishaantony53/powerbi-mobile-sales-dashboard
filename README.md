# Mobile Market Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes mobile phone market performance from 2022–2024 using Power BI. The dashboard leverages descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analytics to identify key sales drivers, evaluate pricing and promotional strategies, and support revenue optimization.

## Objectives

* Analyze sales and revenue trends across mobile brands and categories.
* Identify key factors influencing Units Sold using AI-driven analytics.
* Evaluate the impact of 5G technology and flagship devices on sales performance.
* Compare the effectiveness of pricing and promotional strategies.
* Provide data-driven recommendations for business growth.

## Tools & Techniques

* Power BI
* DAX Measures & KPIs
* Data Modeling
* Key Influencers (ML.NET)
* What-If Parameter Analysis
* Interactive Dashboards
* Predictive & Prescriptive Analytics

## Key Findings

* Flagship smartphones and 5G-enabled devices are the strongest predictors of sales volume.
* A 10% increase in promotional activity increases projected revenue to approximately $5.41 Billion.
* A 10% price reduction decreases projected revenue to approximately $4.25 Billion.
* Samsung and Apple generate the highest sales volumes, while Xiaomi, Oppo, and Huawei show strong growth potential.
* Seasonal sales patterns create opportunities for targeted marketing campaigns.

## Dashboard Features

* Executive Summary Dashboard
* Revenue and Sales Analysis
* Brand Performance Analysis
* Category Performance Analysis
* AI-Powered Key Influencers Analysis
* What-If Scenario Modeling
* Strategic Recommendations Dashboard

## Outcomes

* Demonstrated that promotional investments deliver higher returns than price discounts.
* Identified flagship and 5G smartphones as primary revenue drivers.
* Supported inventory optimization and marketing budget allocation.
* Provided actionable insights for revenue growth and strategic decision-making.

## Business Recommendations

* Prioritize promotional campaigns over broad price reductions.
* Focus inventory and marketing efforts on flagship and 5G-enabled devices.
* Implement channel-specific marketing strategies.
* Optimize product portfolios by reducing low-performing budget models.
* Maintain a stable pricing strategy while increasing product visibility.
